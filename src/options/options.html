Other memory

Notes that live somewhere else. A notes folder (Obsidian or plain Markdown) is read in the browser and never written to; its unchecked - [ ] items become tasks. A pasted memory is sent once, with secrets removed, to Muse Spark 1.3 (not Contributor) and turned into entries. An export file (Claude's conversations.json, or Gemini's MyActivity.json from Google Takeout) is read in the browser; only conversations from the last 30 days in which you name a date or a to-do are sent, at most 50, after you see how many.

What Tuck looked at

    
When you set or finish a goal, or say you are stuck, Tuck looks things up before it answers. These are its last lookups: what it searched for and how much it found, never your notes' text.

    

    

  


  

    
Page reading

    
    
Right before Tuck would nudge you about a page, it checks whether you are typing there (it notes when you last pressed a key, never which key), and reads up to 1,000 characters near where you type, from the conversation you last touched, or from the middle of the window, so a work chat on a social site does not count as drifting. Secrets are removed first. On Meta it goes to Muse Spark 1.3, not Contributor, so it is not used for training. Only the verdict is kept, never the text. Pages with a password field and the sites below are never read.
